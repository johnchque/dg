  1. What is it, in one breath? The description line at the top already does this.
  2. Why did it exist? Who asked, what the song or cause was about, what you wanted to say.
  3. What was the idea? The concept or central choice that makes this project different from a generic version of it.
  4. What did you actually do? Your hands on it, concretely. This is what a Danish employer or collaborator reads for.
  5. How did you make it work? Constraints, decisions and tricks. This shows craft.
  6. What happened after? Release, reactions, numbers if you have them, or simply how it landed.
  7. What did it teach you? One or two honest sentences. This is your reflective voice, and it's what makes it your page rather than a
     press release.
  8. Credits. The people.
