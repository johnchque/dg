Raaz was the first video Vishay Khxl and I made from his album *Maturity is a Scam*. It is a love song, and we wanted the video to carry that feeling of love and longing for what something could be, even when society does not let it happen.

I directed, filmed and edited it.

## The idea

Raaz means secret. The song is about someone he loved who had to stay hidden from the world, but never from him. Instead of a full story, we went for a simple visualizer that carries the melancholy of the song. Vishay walks around Copenhagen as if he were calling the person he loves, and in some parts he tells her what he is singing.

## Production

I used a Sony A7 III with the 28-70mm kit lens, the same gear I later used for You Love My Arrogance, and we recorded around the city on two different days.

Most of the work happened in the edit. I spent a lot of time on the color grading, on the subtitles for the lyrics, and on cutting everything so it matches the song.

## After

The song was very well received on Spotify, and the video got a good reception too.

Unlike You Love My Arrogance, which came later with a full cast and a script, this one was less about directing people and more about editing. It taught me a lot about color grading, subtitles, and making the picture follow the music.
