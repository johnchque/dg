A short trip to Copenhagen. We landed in the evening and the city was already lit up for winter.

![Copenhagen harbor at night](https://ik.imagekit.io/johnchque/website/photos/20241117_cph/cph_1.jpg?width=3499&height=2336 "The view from our hotel window")

The next morning we walked along the water, no real plan, just following whatever looked interesting.

![Copenhagen](https://ik.imagekit.io/johnchque/website/photos/20241117_cph/cph_2.jpg?width=3499&height=2336)

By the afternoon the light had turned soft and low, the kind that makes even ordinary streets look deliberate.

![Copenhagen](https://ik.imagekit.io/johnchque/website/photos/20241117_cph/cph_3.jpg?width=3499&height=2307 "Copenhagen, November 2024.")
