Most of what I've written online over the years lives on servers I don't control, formatted by rules I didn't set, next to ads I didn't choose. That's the deal social platforms offer, and for a long time I took it without thinking much about it.

I'm trying something else here: a small site I own outright, where a photo is just a photo and a paragraph is just a paragraph, not an engagement surface. Slower to build, definitely less convenient than opening an app — but the tradeoff feels worth it.

This is very much a first draft of that thinking. I expect to disagree with parts of this essay in a year.
